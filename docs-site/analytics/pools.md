# Pool Explorer

Pools are Uniswap-style markets on chain 4663, sorted by DexPaprika 24h
volume. Each row is a pool, not a wallet.

Use this view to see where liquidity sits (often WETH or USDG pairs). Click
through to Blockscout or Uniswap to inspect ticks and fees.

HoodFolio does not let you add liquidity. Impermanent loss and fee tiers
are Uniswap mechanics.
