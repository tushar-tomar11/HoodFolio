# Meme coin holdings

The **Meme Coins** app route lists Uniswap pools on Robinhood Chain ranked
by DexPaprika **24h volume**, not a curated "safe memes" list.

## Why this is not a balance sheet

HoodFolio does not invent meme-token balances. If your wallet holds an
unmapped ERC-20, it will not appear in the stock-token portfolio table.
Pool explorer rows are market data.

## How to interpret the list

High 24h volume does not mean the token is an equity or is redeemable.
Contract risk is yours. Always open Blockscout and Uniswap yourself.
